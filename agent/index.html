The AI Pricing Agent uses your Arzimate account. Open the Arzimate website, log in, then click AI Pricing Agent.

Price a BOQ or a specification

Your workbook comes back unchanged, with margin, total and Grand Total columns, a Summary, and a Backup sheet that shows how every rate was built up. Choose several BOQ files to price them together: each comes back as its own workbook.

Panel or substation enquiry without a BOQ? Choose the specification or scope (Word or PDF): you get a full offer pack, a priced offer workbook with BOM (Siemens, Schneider, ABB), compliance matrix and terms, plus an SLD and a GA drawing in PDF for each option or panel.

A whole tender? Choose "Tender" and add all its files (specifications, BOQ, SLDs, makes list): the agent runs your 7 steps in the background and returns one pack with the tender details, the BOQ check, supplier enquiry drafts, technical documents, the costing with its check, and separate technical and commercial offers.

No word limit: a long specification is read in parts.
Add every file of the tender: PDF, Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or Word (.docx), up to 60 files. Supplier enquiries come back as drafts: nothing is sent until you send it. A large tender takes 30 to 60 minutes; you can close this panel and come back. SLD drawings (PDFs with "SLD" or "single line" in the file name) are read for their boards and feeders: you check the boards here before the costing goes on.
Panels and DBs drawn in these SLDs are priced from their feeders. To use them with “Compute with AI Agent”, add them here before computing.
  • Lines priced before are taken from your own past offers and costings.
  • Panels and DBs drawn in the SLDs you add are built up from their feeders; others are priced from your past panel offers, with a CHECK note.
  • Everything else is broken into parts (main item, accessories, supports, tagging, installation) and priced part by part.
  • It runs on the server: you can close this panel and come back; the job appears below.
  • The files you send go to the agent's server to be priced. Only if “Save project files to my Google Drive” is on in the website's Settings, the BOQs you send and the priced workbooks are also saved in your own Google Drive (Arzimate folder).
